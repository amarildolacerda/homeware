# AgriSense IoT — Commercial Pitch (Agribusiness)

**Date**: 2026-07-24
**Product**: Homeware → AgriSense IoT
**Audience**: Farmers, cooperatives, agribusiness integrators

## Positioning

> "AgriSense IoT is a smart monitoring platform for agribusiness — modular wireless sensors that operate independently of the internet, with a local dashboard and optional integration to management systems."

## Pitch

### Problem

Post-harvest losses, inefficient irrigation, electrical failures in silos and poultry houses — all cost money. Available monitoring solutions in the field require stable internet (rare in rural areas) or are expensive and locked down.

### Solution

AgriSense IoT is a modular monitoring platform that works 100% offline. Wireless sensors (200m+ range) measure temperature, humidity, rain, presence, and toxic gases — all battery-powered with months of autonomy. Data is available on a local dashboard accessible via phone or desktop.

### Differentiators

- **Zero cloud dependency**: works without internet — ideal for sheds, silos, and remote areas
- **Plug & Play**: installs in minutes, no network infrastructure required
- **Modular**: start with 1 sensor and expand as needed
- **Remote updates**: new features arrive via OTA, no truck rolls
- **Optional integration**: connects to larger systems (Home Assistant, ERPs) when internet is available

### Use Cases

- Grain silo temperature alert before spoilage
- Rain sensor automatically halts irrigation
- Gas detection in poultry houses
- Motion/presence in supply sheds

### Business Model

Starter kit + individual sensors. Remote technical support. Optional monitoring plan for smart reports.
